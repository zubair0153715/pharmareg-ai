Refund Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Operated by Zubair Ahmed (Pharm-D), Pakistan

Subscription payments are processed by Paddle, acting as the merchant of record. This page explains when refunds apply and how to request one.

1. Audit Cloud SaaS subscriptions

Plans (Professional USD 49/month, Enterprise USD 199/month) are billed monthly and renew automatically until you cancel. You may cancel at any time from your account or by contacting us — cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and your account reverts to the Free plan.

No refunds for partial months: if you cancel mid-cycle, you keep paid access until the period ends but the remaining days are not refunded.

2. Single audits (USD 800–1,200)

Paid single audits are non-refundable once work has begun, because the audit report is produced for your documents specifically. Two exceptions:

  • If we cannot deliver your audit within the stated turnaround (3–5 business days), you may request a full refund.
  • If you cancel before we start work on your documents, you receive a full refund.

3. Free services

The free sample audit, the free self-audit quiz, and the Free SaaS plan involve no payment and therefore no refund.

4. How to request a refund

Write to us with your account email and the payment receipt:

  • WhatsApp: +92 311 6925020
  • Email: za0153715@gmail.com

Approved refunds are issued through Paddle to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.

5. Disputes and chargebacks

If you paid through Paddle and disagree with a refund decision, you may also use Paddle's buyer support and dispute process. Chargebacks filed with your bank or card issuer are handled by Paddle as the merchant of record.

6. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any statutory rights you may have under the consumer laws of your country of residence, including EU/UK withdrawal rights for digital services where they apply.
