Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Operated by Zubair Ahmed (Pharm-D), Pakistan

We collect only what the service needs to work, we do not sell your data, and we do not process patient-identifiable health data.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored hashed) when you register for Audit Cloud or QMS Lite.
  • Audit inputs: answers you enter into self-audits and documents you upload for Document Fixes or QMS records.
  • Lead information: name, company, email, phone/WhatsApp number, and request details you provide in the sample-audit request form.
  • Payment records: subscription status and plan tier, processed by our payment provider (Paddle) as merchant of record. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Basic technical logs: IP address, browser type, and access timestamps for security and reliability.

2. How we use it

To provide, operate, and improve the services; to respond to your requests (including via WhatsApp); to manage accounts, plans, and billing; and to detect misuse or fraud. We do not use your documents for marketing and we do not train third-party AI models on your proprietary data.

3. What we never do

We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone. We do not share your documents with third parties except: (a) with your instruction (e.g., sharing a report link you generate); (b) with service providers strictly needed to run the service (hosting, payment processing); or (c) where required by law.

4. Data retention and deletion

Audit history is kept in your account until you delete it. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, message us on WhatsApp at +92 311 6925020 from your registered number or email za0153715@gmail.com — we will confirm and delete within 7 days. Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before automatic expiry.

5. Security

Passwords are stored hashed (never in plain text), sessions expire automatically, and QMS records are protected by a tamper-evident audit trail. No system is perfect — if you suspect unauthorized access to your account, contact us immediately.

6. Cookies

We use only essential cookies/tokens: login sessions and security. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Your rights

You may ask us at any time what data we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to correct or delete it — via WhatsApp at +92 311 6925020 or email za0153715@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the services after the update constitutes acceptance.
